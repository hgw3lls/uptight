Research context

Choose a frame or location

A finding can support a claim and still leave its camera position uncertain.

What helps most

  • A comparison that shows the same feature in both images.
  • A dated source, with its original archive or publication reference.
  • A clear account of what the evidence establishes and what remains uncertain.

Keep the distinction

A landmark match, a former building’s address and an exact camera position are different claims. A proposed map point stays a proposal until the evidence supports it.
