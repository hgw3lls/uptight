## Modern map context

The bundled Cleveland context was prepared from U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb
Transportation and Hydrography data dated January 1, 2026. Source URLs, query
provenance and attribution are retained in data/cleveland-context.json.

- https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Transportation/MapServer
- https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Hydro/MapServer
- https://www.census.gov/newsroom/archives/2014-pr/cb14-208.html

This is modern geographic context, not a reconstruction of Cleveland in 1968.
